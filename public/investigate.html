BAY AREA PRESS REPOSITORY — SAN FRANCISCO, CA ACCESSION #BAPR-1968-74-Z

PRESS CLIPPINGS FILE: UNSOLVED CASE

SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA — DECEMBER 1968 TO PRESENT
SOURCE: Microfilm reels of the San Francisco Chronicle, San Francisco Examiner, Vallejo Times-Herald, Napa Valley Register, and affiliated regional publications, 1968–1974.

DESCRIPTION: Collection of front-page and regional news clippings related to the unidentified subject known to correspondents as "ZODIAC." Files contain reports on attacks, investigative bulletins, published cipher fragments, and letters received by editorial offices. Materials preserved as originally printed, with editorial markings intact.
Clippings File Case Log

INVESTIGATIVE LOG — KEY DATES

DEC 20 1968: David Faraday, 17, and Betty Lou Jensen, 16, shot to death on Lake Herman Road, Benicia.

JUL 4 1969: Darlene Ferrin killed; Mike Mageau wounded at Blue Rock Springs Park, Vallejo. Subject places phone call to Vallejo PD claiming responsibility.

AUG 1 1969: Three Bay Area newspapers receive identical letters from subject, each containing one-third of a 408-symbol cipher.

AUG 8 1969: Cipher decoded by Salinas schoolteacher Donald Harden and wife Bettye.

SEP 27 1969: Bryan Hartnell and Cecelia Shepard stabbed at Lake Berryessa, Napa County. Shepard dies of injuries 29 SEP.

OCT 11 1969: Cab driver Paul Stine shot in Presidio Heights, SF. Three teen witnesses observe subject.

OCT 1969: Subject threatens school bus children in letter to Chronicle. Armed patrols deployed.

MAR 22 1970: Kathleen Johns reports abduction near Modesto; subject later claims credit.

1970–1974: Continued correspondence received by Chronicle, including additional ciphers and references to victim count.
